Frequently
asked questions

01

Which video editing package is right for me?

It depends on your content goals. Our Starter package is ideal for testing one Meta ad creative, while Growth and Scale are designed for brands that need multiple creatives to test different hooks and angles. For YouTube videos or personal branding content, get in touch for a custom quote.

02

Why should I choose Focus Studio for video editing?

We help brands and creators turn raw footage into polished, engaging content without the hassle of managing the editing process themselves. From ad hooks and captions to pacing, sound design, and storytelling, we focus on creating videos that match your brand and keep your audience watching.

03

How long does it take to receive my edited videos?

Turnaround time depends on the type of content, project complexity, and number of videos. Once we review your requirements and receive your footage, we'll provide an estimated delivery timeline before starting. If you have a deadline, let us know in advance.

04

Can I request changes if the final edit isn't what I expected?

Yes. Our Meta Ads packages include two rounds of revisions per creative. For YouTube editing and personal branding projects, revision terms will be agreed upon before work begins. We also encourage you to share references and clear instructions to help us get the style right from the start.

05

How does the payment process work?

Our Meta Ads packages have clear upfront pricing, starting at $80 for one creative, with discounted rates for larger packages. YouTube and personal branding projects are quoted based on your requirements. We'll confirm the scope, price, and payment terms before beginning your project.

06

How can I send my raw footage and large files?

You can share your footage and project assets through Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, or another file-sharing platform you prefer. Just make sure the files are accessible and include any references, brand assets, or instructions needed for the edit.

07

Do you work with clients outside your country?

Absolutely! Focus Studio works with brands, businesses, YouTubers, and personal brands worldwide. We manage projects remotely through online communication and file sharing, making it easy to collaborate regardless of your location.
